# Finance Module - Simple Explanation Guide

## Overview
The Finance Module helps you track all money coming in and going out of your business. Think of it like a detailed money diary for your company.

---

## 📄 Main Pages Explained

### 1. **Payments & Receipts** (`PaymentsReceiptsPage`)
**What it does:** Records when customers pay you money

**Simple explanation:**
- When a customer pays for an invoice or service, you record it here
- Like a cash register that tracks all payments received
- Shows: Who paid, how much, when, and what payment method (cash, bank transfer, etc.)

**Example:** Customer John pays 50,000 MMK for Invoice #123 → Record it here

---

### 2. **Accounts Receivable** (`AccountsReceivablePage`)
**What it does:** Shows who owes you money

**Simple explanation:**
- Lists all customers who haven't paid yet
- Shows how much each customer owes
- Helps you follow up on unpaid bills

**Example:** 
- Customer A owes 100,000 MMK
- Customer B owes 50,000 MMK
- Total money owed to you: 150,000 MMK

**Think of it as:** Your "money I'm waiting to receive" list

---

### 3. **Accounts Payable** (`AccountsPayablePage`)
**What it does:** Tracks money you owe to suppliers/vendors

**Simple explanation:**
- When you buy something from a supplier, they send you a bill
- This page tracks all bills you need to pay
- Helps you know what you owe and when to pay

**Example:**
- Office supplies vendor: 30,000 MMK (due next week)
- Internet service: 20,000 MMK (due this month)
- Total you need to pay: 50,000 MMK

**Think of it as:** Your "bills to pay" list

---

### 4. **Cash & Treasury** (`CashManagementPage`)
**What it does:** Tracks your actual cash and bank account money

**Simple explanation:**
- Shows how much money you have in bank accounts
- Records when money comes in (inflow) or goes out (outflow)
- Like checking your bank balance, but more detailed

**Example:**
- Bank Account 1: 500,000 MMK
- Bank Account 2: 200,000 MMK
- Cash in office: 50,000 MMK
- Total cash available: 750,000 MMK

**Think of it as:** Your wallet and bank accounts combined

---

### 5. **General Ledger** (`GeneralLedgerPage`)
**What it does:** The master record of all financial transactions

**Simple explanation:**
- Like a detailed accounting book
- Every money movement is recorded here
- Organized by categories (accounts)
- Ensures everything balances (money in = money out)

**Example:**
- Category: "Office Rent" - 100,000 MMK paid
- Category: "Sales Revenue" - 500,000 MMK received
- Everything must balance (debit = credit)

**Think of it as:** The main accounting book that keeps everything organized

---

### 6. **Financial Analytics** (`FinancialAnalyticsPage`)
**What it does:** Shows financial reports and summaries

**Simple explanation:**
- Creates reports showing:
  - How much you earned (Revenue)
  - How much you spent (Expenses)
  - Profit (Revenue - Expenses)
- Shows your financial health at a glance

**Example:**
- Revenue this month: 1,000,000 MMK
- Expenses this month: 600,000 MMK
- Profit: 400,000 MMK

**Think of it as:** Your monthly financial report card

---

### 7. **Fixed Assets** (`FixedAssetsPage`)
**What it does:** Tracks valuable things your company owns

**Simple explanation:**
- Records expensive items that last a long time
- Examples: computers, vehicles, furniture, equipment
- Tracks their value and depreciation (how they lose value over time)

**Example:**
- Laptop: Bought for 500,000 MMK, now worth 300,000 MMK (after 2 years)
- Car: Bought for 5,000,000 MMK, now worth 3,000,000 MMK (after 3 years)

**Think of it as:** Your company's valuable possessions list

---

### 8. **Capital Management** (`CapitalManagementPage`)
**What it does:** Tracks loans, investments, and company ownership

**Simple explanation:**
- Records money borrowed (loans)
- Tracks investments made in the company
- Shows who owns what part of the company

**Example:**
- Bank loan: 2,000,000 MMK (need to pay back)
- Owner investment: 1,000,000 MMK (owner put in money)
- Company equity: 3,000,000 MMK (company's net worth)

**Think of it as:** Your company's borrowing and ownership records

---

## 📋 Document Types

### **Invoices** (`InvoicesTab`)
**What it is:** Official bills you send to customers

**Simple explanation:**
- When you complete work or sell something, you create an invoice
- It tells the customer: "You owe me this much money"
- Has invoice number, amount, due date

**Example:** 
- Invoice #001: "Website Development - 500,000 MMK, Due: Jan 15"

---

### **Quotations** (`QuotationsTab`)
**What it is:** Price estimates you give to potential customers

**Simple explanation:**
- Before doing work, you give a quote (estimate)
- Customer can accept or reject it
- If accepted, you can convert it to an invoice

**Example:**
- Quote #001: "Website Development - 500,000 MMK"
- Status: Sent (waiting for customer response)

---

### **Payments** (`PaymentsTab`)
**What it is:** Records of money received from customers

**Simple explanation:**
- Every time a customer pays, it's recorded here
- Links to the invoice they're paying for
- Shows payment method and date

**Example:**
- Payment #001: 500,000 MMK received for Invoice #001
- Method: Bank Transfer
- Date: Jan 10, 2024

- Money customers owe you

### **Accounts Payable (AP)**
- Money you owe to suppliers
- "Payable" = you need to pay it
- Like your bills to pay

### **Ledger**
- The main accounting book
- Records every transaction
- Keeps everything organized

### **Reconciliation**
- Matching your records with bank statements
- Making sure everything matches
- Like balancing a checkbook

### **Depreciation**
- How expensive items lose value over time
- Example: A car worth 5M today might be worth 3M next year
- Normal wear and tear reduces value

## 🧮 How Financial Calculations Work

This section explains exactly how the system calculates all your financial numbers. Think of it as the "math behind the scenes."

---

### 1. **Client/Business Outstanding Balance Calculation**

**What it calculates:** How much money a customer still owes you

**Formula:**
```
Outstanding Balance = Opening Balance + Total Billed - Total Paid
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Opening Balance** (if set)
   - Starting balance when you first add the client
   - Can be positive (they owe you) or negative (you owe them)
   - Example: 50,000 MMK

2. **Total Billed**
   - Add up ALL invoices and sales for this client
   - Only count invoices/sales that are NOT cancelled
   - Formula: Sum of all invoice amounts + Sum of all sale amounts
   - Example:
     - Invoice #001: 200,000 MMK
     - Invoice #002: 150,000 MMK
     - Sale #001: 100,000 MMK
     - Total Billed = 450,000 MMK

3. **Total Paid**
   - Add up ALL payments received from this client
   - Formula: Sum of all payment amounts
   - Example:
     - Payment #001: 200,000 MMK
     - Payment #002: 100,000 MMK
     - Total Paid = 300,000 MMK

4. **Final Calculation:**
   ```
   Outstanding = 50,000 (opening) + 450,000 (billed) - 300,000 (paid)
   Outstanding = 200,000 MMK
   ```

**Real Example:**
- Client starts with 0 opening balance
- You send 3 invoices: 100K + 200K + 150K = 450K total
- Client pays: 200K + 100K = 300K total
- Outstanding: 0 + 450K - 300K = **150,000 MMK still owed**

---

### 2. **Total Revenue Calculation**

**What it calculates:** All money your company earned

**Formula:**
```
Total Revenue = Client Payments + Training Payments + Other Income
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Client Revenue**
   - All payments from business clients
   - Formula: Sum of all payment amounts from clients
   - Example: 2,000,000 MMK

2. **Training Revenue**
   - All payments from students/courses
   - Formula: Sum of all student payment amounts
   - Example: 500,000 MMK

3. **Other Income**
   - Any other money coming in
   - Example: 100,000 MMK

4. **Final Calculation:**
   ```
   Total Revenue = 2,000,000 + 500,000 + 100,000
   Total Revenue = 2,600,000 MMK
   ```

---

### 3. **Total Expenses Calculation**

**What it calculates:** All money your company spent

**Formula:**
```
Total Expenses = Operating Expenses + Payroll + Visa Reloads + Other Costs
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Operating Expenses**
   - Office rent, utilities, supplies, etc.
   - Formula: Sum of all expense amounts
   - Example: 300,000 MMK

2. **Payroll**
   - Employee salaries for the period
   - Formula: Sum of all employee net pay
   - Example: 1,000,000 MMK

3. **Visa Reloads**
   - Company credit card reloads
   - Formula: Sum of all visa reload amounts
   - Example: 200,000 MMK

4. **Other Costs**
   - Any other expenses
   - Example: 100,000 MMK

5. **Final Calculation:**
   ```
   Total Expenses = 300,000 + 1,000,000 + 200,000 + 100,000
   Total Expenses = 1,600,000 MMK
   ```

---

### 4. **Profit/Loss Calculation**

**What it calculates:** Did you make money or lose money?

**Formula:**
```
Profit (or Loss) = Total Revenue - Total Expenses
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. Get Total Revenue (from calculation #2)
2. Get Total Expenses (from calculation #3)
3. Subtract: Revenue - Expenses

**Examples:**

**Example 1: Making Profit**
- Revenue: 2,600,000 MMK
- Expenses: 1,600,000 MMK
- Profit = 2,600,000 - 1,600,000 = **1,000,000 MMK** ✅

**Example 2: Losing Money**
- Revenue: 1,000,000 MMK
- Expenses: 1,500,000 MMK
- Loss = 1,000,000 - 1,500,000 = **-500,000 MMK** ❌

**Example 3: Breaking Even**
- Revenue: 2,000,000 MMK
- Expenses: 2,000,000 MMK
- Profit = 0 MMK (no profit, no loss)

---

### 5. **Invoice Total Calculation**

**What it calculates:** Final amount on an invoice

**Formula:**
```
Invoice Total = Subtotal - Discounts + Tax + Other Fees
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Subtotal**
   - Add up all line items
   - Formula: Sum of (Quantity × Unit Price) for each item
   - Example:
     - Item 1: 5 × 20,000 = 100,000 MMK
     - Item 2: 3 × 30,000 = 90,000 MMK
     - Subtotal = 190,000 MMK

2. **Discounts**
   - Any discounts applied
   - Example: 10,000 MMK discount

3. **Tax**
   - Tax percentage on subtotal
   - Formula: (Subtotal - Discount) × Tax Rate
   - Example: (190,000 - 10,000) × 5% = 9,000 MMK

4. **Other Fees**
   - Additional charges
   - Example: 1,000 MMK

5. **Final Calculation:**
   ```
   Invoice Total = 190,000 - 10,000 + 9,000 + 1,000
   Invoice Total = 190,000 MMK
   ```

---

### 6. **Accounts Receivable Total**

**What it calculates:** Total money owed by all customers

**Formula:**
```
Total AR = Sum of (Each Client's Outstanding Balance)
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. Get outstanding balance for Client A
2. Get outstanding balance for Client B
3. Get outstanding balance for Client C
4. Add them all together

**Example:**
- Client A owes: 200,000 MMK
- Client B owes: 150,000 MMK
- Client C owes: 50,000 MMK
- Client D owes: 0 MMK (paid everything)
- **Total AR = 400,000 MMK**

---

### 7. **Accounts Payable Total**

**What it calculates:** Total money you owe to all suppliers

**Formula:**
```
Total AP = Sum of (Each Vendor's Outstanding Balance)
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. Get outstanding balance for Vendor A
2. Get outstanding balance for Vendor B
3. Add them all together

**Example:**
- Vendor A (Office Supplies): 30,000 MMK
- Vendor B (Internet): 20,000 MMK
- Vendor C (Rent): 0 MMK (paid)
- **Total AP = 50,000 MMK**

---

### 8. **Cash Balance Calculation**

**What it calculates:** How much cash you actually have

**Formula:**
```
Current Cash Balance = Starting Balance + All Inflows - All Outflows
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Starting Balance**
   - Cash at beginning of period
   - Example: 500,000 MMK

2. **Inflows** (Money Coming In)
   - Customer payments
   - Loans received
   - Investments
   - Example: 1,000,000 MMK

3. **Outflows** (Money Going Out)
   - Supplier payments
   - Salaries
   - Expenses
   - Example: 600,000 MMK

4. **Final Calculation:**
   ```
   Current Balance = 500,000 + 1,000,000 - 600,000
   Current Balance = 900,000 MMK
   ```

---

### 9. **Depreciation Calculation** (Fixed Assets)

**What it calculates:** How much value an asset loses over time

**Common Method: Straight-Line Depreciation**

**Formula:**
```
Annual Depreciation = (Purchase Price - Salvage Value) ÷ Useful Life (years)
Current Value = Purchase Price - (Annual Depreciation × Years Owned)
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Purchase Price**
   - What you paid for it
   - Example: 5,000,000 MMK (car)

2. **Salvage Value**
   - What it's worth at the end
   - Example: 500,000 MMK (after 5 years)

3. **Useful Life**
   - How many years it lasts
   - Example: 5 years

4. **Calculate Annual Depreciation:**
   ```
   Annual = (5,000,000 - 500,000) ÷ 5
   Annual = 900,000 MMK per year
   ```

5. **Current Value After 2 Years:**
   ```
   Current = 5,000,000 - (900,000 × 2)
   Current = 3,200,000 MMK
   ```

---

### 10. **Company Net Worth (Equity) Calculation**

**What it calculates:** What your company is actually worth

**Formula:**
```
Net Worth (Equity) = Total Assets - Total Liabilities
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Total Assets**
   - Cash: 900,000 MMK
   - Fixed Assets: 3,200,000 MMK
   - Accounts Receivable: 400,000 MMK
   - Total Assets = 4,500,000 MMK

2. **Total Liabilities**
   - Accounts Payable: 50,000 MMK
   - Loans: 1,000,000 MMK
   - Total Liabilities = 1,050,000 MMK

3. **Final Calculation:**
   ```
   Net Worth = 4,500,000 - 1,050,000
   Net Worth = 3,450,000 MMK
   ```

## ⚠️ Important Notes About Calculations

### 1. **Opening Balance**
- Set when you first add a client
- Can be positive (they owe you) or negative (you owe them)
- Only added once, at the beginning

### 2. **Cancelled Invoices**
- Cancelled invoices are NOT counted in Total Billed
- This prevents inflating the amount owed

### 3. **Payment Matching**
- Payments can be linked to specific invoices
- Or can be "general" payments (not linked to any invoice)
- Both count toward Total Paid

### 4. **Balance Updates**
- Client balance updates automatically when:
  - New invoice created → Balance increases
  - Payment received → Balance decreases
  - Invoice cancelled → Balance decreases

### 5. **Date Ranges**
- Reports can filter by date range
- Only transactions in that range are counted
- Useful for monthly/quarterly reports

## 💡 Understanding Negative Numbers

Sometimes you'll see negative numbers. Here's what they mean:

- **Negative Outstanding Balance** = You owe the customer money (overpayment or credit)
- **Negative Profit** = You lost money (expenses > revenue)
- **Negative Cash** = You have debt/overdraft (spent more than you have)

---

## 🔍 How to Verify Calculations

If numbers seem wrong, check:

1. **Opening Balance** - Is it set correctly?
2. **All Invoices** - Are they all counted? (except cancelled ones)
3. **All Payments** - Are they all recorded?
4. **Date Filters** - Are you looking at the right time period?
5. **Currency** - Are all amounts in the same currency (MMK)?

---

This system automatically calculates everything for you, but understanding the math helps you verify the numbers and catch any mistakes!
